What Is a Chronikin?

Creatures born from myths and legends.

The world is full of myths and legends from the many cultures that inhabit it. From the marbled temples of Deolis to the nomadic tribes of the Johaalgi Plains, the stories of the people inhabiting the land have proliferated generation after generation.

At some point in history, a still unknown phenomenon led to the appearance of creatures that defied nature, appearing around the many places where these myths originated. These were impossible creatures that seemed tied to the very stories used to describe natural phenomena or teach important lessons.

History recalls a period of great turmoil, as many people did not know how to react to these strange creatures. Their natures seemed as varied as their appearances. Though some were reminiscent of the fauna inhabiting the land and sea, others appeared entirely unnatural and defied current understanding.

It was eventually discovered that certain people were able to bond with these creatures, now called Chronikin, and that through shared experiences they could grow and understand one another. Hundreds of years later, several societies have been completely altered by the integration of Chronikin and their partners, known as Archivists.

Chronikin are the magical creatures that form the central narrative focus of Chronicles of Myth. They are fantastical entities that hail from a realm beyond our own and are derived from real-world myths and legends. A Chronikin may encompass a single myth or comprise elements from several.

Archivists utilize the Chronikin they have bonded with to accomplish feats that would otherwise be impossible. As Chronikin travel with their Archivists, they grow in both strength and stature, changing forms and unlocking new and powerful traits. The Chronikin an Archivist chooses as partners, and how those companions are raised, is entirely left to the player.

Chronikin Allies

A bond shared in spirit

There are many Chronikin that inhabit the world. Every Archivist chooses their first companion at the beginning of their journey, but any potential allies they meet afterward must be made along the way. This happens through the art of Chronikin bonding.

Bonding is the term used to describe the link between an Archivist and a Chronikin. The process is not fully understood, but it entails the spirits of the two joining together harmoniously and symbiotically. The things the Archivist experiences and feels are shared by the Chronikin, and vice versa.

An Archivist may travel with up to three bonded Chronikin during their journey. However, a single Archivist may bond with any number of Chronikin they encounter.

When an Archivist is already traveling with three Chronikin companions, the essence of any additional bonded Chronikin is stored within the Archivist's Chronikin Chronicles. This special book is given to all Archivists as a means of studying these wondrous creatures, and players begin their journey with one in their possession.

Myths Taking Form

Ascension and transformation

Chronikin hail from an entirely different world. As such, their nature and very being are alien to those residing within the world of Chronicles of Myth. Their appearances are linked to the many myths and legends that make up the surrounding cultures.

Most Chronikin arrive in a diminished form because of the stress caused by traveling from their world. When a Chronikin obtains essence from a font of magic or through traveling with a capable Archivist, its form may change until it returns to what is believed to be its true state: a more complete and fantastical creature that transcends reality.

Within Chronicles of Myth, these forms represent more than a change in appearance. A Chronikin's strength, Hallmarks, battle techniques, and even its Archetype can change when a new form is achieved.

Some Chronikin also develop expanded forms of movement. When an Archivist is sufficiently skilled, these companions may ferry their bonded partners across the land, sea, or even the sky.

The three known stages of Chronikin ascension are Fractured, Balanced, and Fabled.

The Twelve Aspects

The forces that shape a Chronikin's essence.

Aspects are the magical powers that Chronikin use. They influence a Chronikin's appearance, traits, behavior, and actions. The appearance of Chronikin has also had a profound effect on the surrounding environment, allowing their magical energies to influence the people, creatures, plants, and constructs of the world.

01

Martial

This Aspect embodies physical prowess and aptitude. Chronikin of this Aspect typically fight with a sense of technique and purposeful intent. Some even employ tools or weapons. They are born from the many myths of legendary heroes and battles.

02

Beast

One of the most common Aspects, Beast is realized through the many animalistic features a Chronikin may possess. Horns, fangs, venom, claws, and other natural weapons can be deployed ferociously. Beast Chronikin also reflect the many behaviors and characteristics of the animal kingdom beyond pure aggression.

03

Mystic

This Aspect is linked to the mind and its untapped potential. The traits of Mystic Chronikin can appear magical, though their actions are accomplished through willpower and unlocked mental capabilities. Myths of prophets and seers have given rise to unnatural clairvoyance, the reading of minds, and visions of possible futures.

04

Geo

This Aspect encompasses the dirt, stones, and minerals that comprise the natural world. The phenomena occurring beneath the planet's surface represent both the devastating force and protective crust displayed by Geo Chronikin.

05

Flame

Chronikin of this Aspect use fire or possess flames upon their bodies. Some manifestations are subtle, while others exist in a constant state of unimaginable heat. Flame may appear as fire, smoke, or magma. Though typically destructive, it may also have restorative qualities through purging and cleansing.

06

Water

Possibly the most numerous Aspect because of its potential habitat, Water Chronikin are always at least semiaquatic. Even fully aquatic Chronikin can traverse land, though some do so more easily than others. Their traits may utilize fresh water, salt water, ice, or steam.

07

Tempest

An extremely chaotic Aspect, Tempest Chronikin use wind, lightning, and hail to affect the battlefield, their opponents, and their allies. These Chronikin can be exceptionally difficult to bond with because of their naturally chaotic dispositions.

08

Nature

This Aspect is sometimes misrepresented as purely plant based. While that is true for some Chronikin, Nature incorporates all forms of growth. Grasping vines, healing fruit, and poisonous fungi demonstrate how diverse this Aspect can be, helping or hindering according to how it is used.

09

Cosmic

This Aspect is characterized by its connection to the vast skies above. Stars and constellations are the sources of myths throughout the world, and Chronikin born from them display otherworldly traits. Meteors, the sun, the moon, and the stars may all be utilized by Chronikin of this Aspect.

10

Nether

This Aspect embodies myths and legends of ghosts, spirits, and the other side. Though not always malicious, Nether Chronikin may appear ghastly or ghoulish and are often feared. Their traits may incorporate void-like powers capable of sapping strength or instilling fear.

11

Divine

A deeply metaphysical Aspect, Divine Chronikin often embody spirituality or humanity's understanding of higher powers. They can appear among the most supernatural of all Chronikin and are frequently linked to legends of creation, divinity, or the afterlife.

12

Prosaic

The appearance of Chronikin has profoundly affected their surrounding environments. Their magical energies have infused the flora and fauna of the world. People, creatures, plants, and even constructs influenced by this energy may obtain magical attributes and abilities. This influence has been termed Prosaic, somewhat ironically. Some researchers believe an Archivist's ability to bond with Chronikin may be connected to an abundance of this energy.
