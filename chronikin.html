The living myths

Meet the Chronikin.

Ethereal beings shaped by humanity's legends, natural wonders, fears, gods, monsters, and heroes.

First bonds

Every partnership begins with trust.

Archivists do not simply collect Chronikin. They bond with them. This connection is symbiotic, allowing the pair to share power, confront danger, and grow through experience.

Chronikin possess Hallmarks such as Muscle, Spirit, Hustle, and Grit, along with Aspects, archetypes, movement types, Essence Points, resistances, and battle techniques.
