Archivists

No one is born a hero.

An Archivist is a person capable of bonding with Chronikin. The life they lived before adventure, represented by their Expertise, shapes the talents, instincts, and choices they bring to the journey.

What Is Expertise?

Your life before the legend.

No one is born a hero. Popular characters throughout stories and legends often have complete lives before they are thrust into adventure. Think of an Expertise as your character's vocation or occupation before the event that placed them upon the path of an Archivist. It represents their experiences leading up to the beginning of their adventure.

There are twelve Expertises to choose from. Your choice determines many of the unique actions, skills, and bonuses your Archivist will have at their disposal as the character progresses through the game world.

Each Expertise is composed of three Aptitude Ranks: Novice, Apprentice, and Professional. As your Archivist grows in experience, you may continue within the same Expertise to unlock new ranks and additional Aptitude Traits.

Every Expertise is also divided into four separate Lifepaths. Each Lifepath contains three Skill Tiers: Beginner, Adept, and Master. These skills directly affect the game, and players are encouraged to employ them in unique and creative ways.

Players may specialize within a single Expertise or branch into different Expertises and Lifepaths at certain points during character progression.

Finally, each Expertise is linked to a different Chronikin Archetype. This connection affects the modifiers and bonuses an Archivist may receive during play. A player may maximize a focused specialization or incorporate skills drawn from many different backgrounds.

Character Philosophy

Specialize or branch out.

Your Expertise represents who your Archivist was before the journey began, but it does not have to define who they will eventually become.

Develop a focused specialization, combine skills from unexpected backgrounds, or allow the events of the story to reshape your Archivist's path.

Twelve Expertises

Choose the life that shaped you.

Each card presents an Expertise overview and its four Lifepaths.

01

Athlete

The Athlete prides themselves on physical ability and constantly pushes their body to the limit. Whether they were involved in organized sports or simply loved a challenging trek, every mountain, river, or cross-country journey is another opportunity for improvement. Athletes train both body and mind. Decisions requiring immediate action are second nature, and they are always searching for a tactical advantage.

Track and FieldClimberLifterMartial Artist

02

Diplomat

The Diplomat is a natural orator and persuader. They are skilled at mediating conflict, and their insight into complex interactions extends beyond that of most people. Whether raised around politics or involved in local councils, they wield words like a skilled smith wields a hammer, constructing arguments capable of winning over even steadfast opposition. Their preferred path is one without violence, where words overcome fists and victory ends with a handshake.

AristocratConciliatorAdvocatorBroker

03

Entertainer

The Entertainer is a performer at heart, and the world is their stage. Whether an aspiring actor, practiced gymnast, musician, dancer, or poet, they have always captured the attention of others. Their performances inspire emotions that border on the supernatural. They never shy away from the spotlight and welcome every opportunity to showcase their talents. Their story is a show, and they are the main attraction.

Innovator

The Innovator sees the world as a collection of working pieces and seeks to fit them together. Whether raised by an architect or trained as a mechanic's apprentice, they understand how things work. That understanding does not leave them content with simply repairing what already exists. They constantly seek to make things better, whether improving Chronikin equipment or creating entirely new gear for unusual situations.

Their understanding of medicinal items and the anatomy of both people and Chronikin gives them an advantage when caring for their companions.
