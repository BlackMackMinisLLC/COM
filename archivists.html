Archivists

No one is born a hero.

An Archivist is a person capable of bonding with Chronikin. The life they lived before adventure—their Expertise—shapes the talents, instincts, and choices they bring to the journey.

What is Expertise?

Your life before the legend.

Think of an Expertise as your character’s vocation or job before the event that set them on the path of an Archivist. It reflects their experiences before the adventure begins.

There are twelve Expertises. Each is composed of three aptitude ranks—Novice, Apprentice, and Professional—and four Lifepaths with their own skill tiers. As an Archivist grows, players may specialize deeply or branch into different backgrounds.

Each Expertise is also linked to a Chronikin Archetype, creating new combinations of traits, modifiers, and choices.

Character Philosophy

Specialize or branch out.

Chronicles of Myth encourages players to employ skills in unique and creative ways. Build toward a focused identity, combine unexpected backgrounds, or let the journey reshape who your Archivist becomes.
