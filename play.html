Campaign role-playing

CoM: Core

Build an Archivist, choose an origin and expertise, form bonds with Chronikin, and take part in long-form adventures shaped by the group.

  • Character-driven campaigns
  • Exploration and discovery
  • Archivist and Chronikin bonds
  • Game Master–led storytelling

Take your forces directly into strategic encounters designed for faster sessions and competitive or casual play.

  • Chronikin-centered strategy
  • Standalone battle format
