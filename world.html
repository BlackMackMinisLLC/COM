The setting

A world altered by living myth.

Human history changed forever when mysterious beings began appearing across the land—beings shaped by the myths and legends people had used to explain nature, heroism, fear, and the divine.

The arrival of the Chronikin

Creatures of unknown origin appeared without warning, reshaping ecosystems, cultures, warfare, faith, trade, and humanity's understanding of reality. Their forms echo familiar stories, but they are living entities with their own personalities and powers.

The Archivists

Only a small portion of humanity possesses the spiritual capacity to bond with Chronikin. These individuals became known as Archivists: explorers, protectors, scholars, combatants, and witnesses charged with understanding the beings now sharing their world.

Origin nations

Four starting cultures. Four ways to see the world.

Deolis

A prosperous empire of marble temples, city-states, scholars, artists, soldiers, and a vast pantheon. Inspired by the ancient Mediterranean world.

Norfjal

A harsh northern land of mountains, forests, frozen lakes, nomadic tribes, master seafarers, and fierce traditions of strength and family.

Tengippon

An island nation shaped by clan conflict, naval power, disciplined warfare, spiritual practice, artistic expression, and the difficult pursuit of unity.

Zharakar

A wealthy crossroads of trade, astronomy, engineering, desert nomads, fertile riverlands, monumental cities, and competing visions of tradition and progress.

The wider world

Beyond the four origin nations lie many additional cultures and territories waiting to be expanded through future adventures, supplements, maps, and archival entries.
