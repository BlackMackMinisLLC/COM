A Land Altered by Myth and Magic

The world of Chronicles of Myth has been forever changed by the introduction of Chronikin and the elemental influence that forms them. The Aspects they possess bleed out from their very presence, altering the landscape and forever changing the environment.

Will you help bring back order and balance, or revel in the chaos, seeking its power for your own?

Origin Nations

Four places to begin a legend.

Origin Nation

Deolis

Tall, marbled columns, massive temples, and decadent bath houses are common sights in the temperate lands of Deolis. The region consists of several city-states, each with a particular focus such as education or combat and a corresponding deity. Rich in resources and culture, Deolis has produced countless works of art, scholarly prose, stories, epics, and parables.

Origin Nation

Norfjal

A harsh province of snowy mountains, vast pine forests, and frozen lakes, Norfjal is the pinnacle of the untamed wilds. Its people are as hardy and fierce as the land they call home. Mostly composed of nomadic tribes, the nation has given rise to legends of heroic gods, villainous powers, and legendary beasts roaming beyond the firelight.

Origin Nation

Amajima

Amajima is a nation defined by conflict. Once composed of hundreds of clans, most have been united through persuasion or force beneath a newly established Imperial aristocracy. Trade and mainland customs have spread, though independent clans still fight tirelessly against assimilation and what they see as the erasure of their identity.

Origin Nation

Zharakar

Rich with history and scientific advancement, Zharakar is a fertile nation beset by harsh deserts and rocky mountains. Its rivers support thriving settlements and a prosperous capital. Positioned between eastern and western nations, it has become a preeminent world power, though many traditional citizens fear that ancient ways are being lost.
