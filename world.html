The records contained within this Archive are still being gathered. CoM: Chronicles of Myth is an actively evolving tabletop role-playing game. New lore, Chronikin, nations, adventures, artwork, and features will be added as development continues.

The known world

Every nation records myth differently.

Some cultures revere the Chronikin. Others fear them, study them, weaponize them, or weave them into daily life. Archivists travel through these lands as witnesses, guardians, scholars, and adventurers.
