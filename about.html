About

Two creators. One shared world.

How it began

From a base theater to a game table.

Wesley and Jeremy met at a military base in Arizona. Both served in the United States Air Force, but instead of meeting on the flightline, they met while volunteering with the base theater group.

After many hours discussing their passions, including the familiar interests shared by many lifelong nerds, they formed a Dungeons & Dragons group with their wives and friends. From that table, the idea for Chronicles of Myth was born, melding their combined passions into a singular project.

Looking Forward

A world designed to grow.

Their sights are set on future books, adventures, artwork, player resources, and a living community of players whose stories will become part of the larger Chronicles of Myth journey.

A Letter from Jeremy

Chronicles of Myth is the culmination of the many things I love and hold dear, distilled into one of my favorite forms of entertainment medium: the TTRPG.

From a young age I have always been fascinated with history and the different legends born out of the questions that early man had. This passion has only grown throughout the years, fed by games like Age of Empires, the God of War series, movies like Clash of the Titans and the Northman, shows such as Samurai Jack and many, many more.

Our goal for Chronicles of Myth is to provide a TTRPG experience like no other. One that lives and breathes old world myths with the fun of creature collection and adventure.

We look forward to the stories we hear from those playing our game and want nothing more than to bring fun and joy to your game table.

Happy gaming!

A Letter from Wesley

Creating Chronicles of Myth with Jeremy has been one of the best journey’s of my life.

Not only do I get to play a TTRPG that I helped create but I get to share it with others that have the same passion and drive to play a TTRPG like no other.

Chronicles of Myth to me shines a new light on one of my favorite genres, a creature capture game with endless possibilities.

Similar to my friend Jeremy, I grew up loving RTS, Sandbox, and Open world games. However, I didn’t start playing TTRPG games until I met Jeremy and he invited me to be a player in one of his games.

Ever since then I have been obsessed with TTRPGs.

After years of making homebrews to fit what we wanted in a game, Jeremy and I finally decided to make our own game.

Now I can’t wait to hear about the amazing adventures people have made playing Chronicles of Myth.
