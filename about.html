Archivist's Note

The records contained within this Archive are still being gathered. CoM: Chronicles of Myth is an actively evolving tabletop role-playing game. New lore, Chronikin, nations, adventures, artwork, and features will be added as development continues.

About the project

About CoM

CoM: Chronicles of Myth is an original tabletop universe built around living myths, the people who document them, and the legends created at the table.

The idea

Myths made real.

The setting begins with a powerful premise: the stories, creatures, and cultural legends once thought imaginary have become part of the living world.

The players

You are the Archivists.

Players step into the role of people capable of forming bonds with Chronikin and recording the extraordinary events that follow.

Website purpose

A public window into the world.

comtabletop.com is being developed as the official digital home of CoM. It will provide public lore, creature previews, news, downloads, and product information without replacing the full published game materials.

As the project matures, the Archive will continue to expand alongside the tabletop game.

“The Archive is never complete. Every legend recorded, every Chronikin discovered, and every adventure shared brings us one page closer to preserving the Chronicles.”
