About

Two creators. One shared world.

Chronicles of Myth began with friendship, theater, military service, and years of tabletop storytelling.

How it began

From a base theater to a game table.

Wesley and Jeremy met at a military base in Arizona. Both served in the United States Air Force, but instead of meeting on the flightline, they met while volunteering with the base theater group.

After many hours discussing their passions, including the familiar interests shared by many lifelong nerds, they formed a Dungeons & Dragons group with their wives and friends. From that table, the idea for Chronicles of Myth was born, melding their combined passions into a singular project.

Looking Forward

A world designed to grow.

Chronicles of Myth seeks to become something greater than a mere indie tabletop RPG. We at BlackMackMinis have lofty ambitions and the drive to see them come to fruition. Future game content is planned with many expansions of the ruleset and settings in the works. Supplementary materials are also being developed with the player in mind, such as a companion app and literary works that expand the world of Chronicles of Myth. We look forward to providing an immersive experience that goes far beyond the gaming table.

A Letter from Jeremy

Chronicles of Myth is the culmination of the many things I love and hold dear, distilled into one of my favorite entertainment mediums: the tabletop role-playing game.

From a young age, I have always been fascinated with history and the different legends born from the questions early people asked about the world. That passion has only grown through games, films, and stories such as Age of Empires, God of War, Clash of the Titans, The Northman, Samurai Jack, and many more.

Our goal is to provide a tabletop experience like no other—one that lives and breathes old-world myths while embracing the fun of creature collection and adventure. We look forward to hearing the stories created by those who play our game, and we want nothing more than to bring fun and joy to your table.

Happy gaming!

A Letter from Wesley

Creating Chronicles of Myth with Jeremy has been one of the best journeys of my life. Not only do I get to play a tabletop game I helped create, but I also get to share it with others who have the same passion and drive to experience something unlike any other TTRPG.

Chronicles of Myth shines a new light on one of my favorite genres: creature capture with endless possibilities. Like Jeremy, I grew up loving strategy, sandbox, and open-world games. I did not begin playing tabletop role-playing games until Jeremy invited me into one of his campaigns—and ever since, I have been obsessed.

After years of creating homebrew material to fit what we wanted from a game, we finally decided to make our own. Now I cannot wait to hear about the amazing adventures players create within Chronicles of Myth.
