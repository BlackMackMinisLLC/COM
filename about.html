The creators

BlackMackMinis

Chronicles of Myth was designed by Jeremy McCalmon and Wesley Blackmon.

Building a world where every battle tells a story.

BlackMackMinis is developing Chronicles of Myth as both a full tabletop roleplaying game and a tactical Skirmish experience. The project combines creature-driven combat, ancient-world inspiration, original lore, and a system built around the bond between Archivists and Chronikin.
